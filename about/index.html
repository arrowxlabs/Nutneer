01 / OUR STORY

Two words.
One honest promise.

NeerThe sacred, life-giving water.

NutThe pure, nutrient-packed seed nature yields.

Rooted deep in the wetlands of the historic Mithila region—the heartland of the world’s finest fox nuts—NeerNut was founded with a singular, honest vision: to take India’s ancient superfood straight from pristine local ponds to mindful homes everywhere, without compromise.

For generations, makhana has been revered across India as an offering of purity during auspicious rituals and fasting. Beyond tradition, it is a naturally balanced superfood: rich in plant protein, packed with essential minerals, light on the stomach, and naturally gluten-free.

At NeerNut, we bridge this timeless regional heritage with modern wellness, creating snacks that honor your health as much as your palate.
