03 / POND TO PALM

The journey
of a pearl.

Months of patient, skilled work by Mithila’s pond families. Every step matters, from the muddy pond bed to the first delicate crunch.

  1. 01

    Sown in the pokhar

    Seeds rest on the muddy bed of shallow ponds; by spring, broad floating leaves cover the water.

  2. 02

    Dived & gathered

    From August to October, skilled divers collect the fallen seeds from the pond floor by hand.

  3. 03

    Washed & sun-dried

    Seeds are cleaned, sieved and dried under the Bihar sun, then graded by size.

  4. 04

    Roasted & popped

    Roasted over wood fire and struck with a wooden mallet, the hard shell cracks and a white pearl pops out.

  5. 05

    To your hand

    From a Darbhanga pond straight to your pantry—pure, light and crunchy.

04 / EVERYDAY WELLNESS

A small pearl.
A little more goodness.

Four quiet strengths of a seed grown in Mithila’s ponds.

Sustained Energy & Satiety

Complex carbohydrates and dietary fiber provide sustained energy. A low glycemic index makes makhana a thoughtful addition to balanced meals and mindful snacking.

Cardiac & Bone Health

Naturally low in sodium, with potassium and magnesium—minerals that contribute to normal blood pressure, heart function and bone health.

Anti-Aging Antioxidants

Contains flavonoids such as kaempferol, antioxidants that help protect cells against oxidative stress.

Digestive Friendly

Naturally gluten-free and light on the stomach. A wholesome choice for fasting (Vrat), everyday snacking or post-workout recovery.
